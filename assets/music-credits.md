# Somewhere, with you

An original, synthesized 48-second ambient loop created for this project. Gentle sine-wave pads, a quiet drone, and bell-like notes; no sampled commercial recordings or external streaming services. You may use, replace, or remove this included track with the website.

Replace `music.mp3` with your own legally usable MP3, keeping the filename. Playback is always opt-in. The player catches missing-file and playback failures so the story remains usable without music.

The five JPEGs are original, abstract illustrated photo placeholders, not photographs of James or Julie. Replace them with your own photographs, retaining `photo1.jpg` through `photo5.jpg`.
